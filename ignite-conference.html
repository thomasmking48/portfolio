Phosphorus Ignite Conference

Phosphorus Cybersecurity's annual conference held in Nashville, TN.

The Project

In 2022, I led the creative development for the launch of a brand-new internal company conference, set to take place in Nashville, TN. This project included building the event's visual identity from the ground up, starting with initial concept development and culminating in a fully realized brand. I created multiple logo concepts and supporting brand elements that captured the energy and intent of the event, which was themed around inspiration and momentum.

The final logo—featured on this page—centers around a stylized flame enclosed within the Phosphorus rectangle, a visual nod to the conference’s title, Ignite. My goal was to design something clean and modern, yet versatile enough to scale across various materials, both digital and print. The flame became a unifying visual motif that I carried through additional collateral, including signage, name badges, digital screens, and promotional materials.

In addition to branding, I developed a series of custom PowerPoint templates tailored to each presenter’s content and presentation style. These templates were designed to align with the overall conference theme, while providing flexibility for diverse subject matter. This work required a blend of strategic thinking, visual storytelling, and close collaboration with stakeholders to ensure every detail reflected the spirit of the event.



Presentations

Each year, I was responsible for designing and producing custom presentation materials tailored to each individual presenter. This process began with the development of a new presentation template aligned with the annual conference theme, ensuring consistency in branding and visual identity. Presenters would submit their content, and I would collaborate with them to transform their ideas into compelling visual narratives. This involved crafting custom slide decks—often featuring detailed visuals, data-driven graphics, and impactful imagery—to help bring each presenter’s message to life and keep the audience engaged. It was a highly collaborative and creative process that called for a strong sense of visual design, a thoughtful approach to storytelling, and the flexibility to work across a variety of subjects and presentation styles.
